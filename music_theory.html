AI music

Today, people can use a streaming app, and when they hit the next button, the app learns which songs or which types of songs to play more often. In the future, there will be apps that generate music by traversing different types of beats and musical progressions or styles. The user will press the continue button to speed up the transition to a different part of the song or different kind of sound. The AI will learn from this feedback about what kind of song to generate.
